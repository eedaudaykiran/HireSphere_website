🔐 Reset your password

Get back into your account quickly and securely

✨ Why it matters — Forgot your password? Locked out? Resetting your password takes less than 2 minutes and keeps your account protected. Follow the steps below to regain access.

📘 Step‑by‑step guide

  • Go to the login page — Click on “Sign in” or visit our login screen at app.example.com/login.
  • Select “Forgot password?” — Below the password field, you’ll see a link that says “Forgot password?”. Click it.
  • Enter your email address — Type the email you used to create your account. Make sure there are no typos.
  • Check your inbox — Within 1–2 minutes, you’ll receive a password reset email from us. (If not, check spam/junk folder.)
  • Click the reset link — The link expires in 60 minutes for security. Click it to open a secure reset page.
  • Create a new password — Use a strong, unique password (at least 8 characters, mix of letters/numbers). Confirm it, then save.
  • Log in with your new password — You’re all set! Return to the login screen and use your updated credentials.

✅ The reset page will show a form with fields for “New password” and “Confirm password”.

❓ Frequently asked questions

I didn’t receive the reset email — what should I do?
✔️ First, wait 2–3 minutes and check your spam/junk folder. Also make sure you typed the correct email address. If you still don’t see it, request another reset link or contact support.
How long is the reset link valid?
✔️ For security, the link expires after 60 minutes. If it expires, simply request a new password reset.
Can I use a previous password again?
✔️ For safety, our system prevents reuse of your last 3 passwords. Please choose a new password you haven't used recently.
What if I’m locked out of my email account?
✔️ You’ll need to recover access to your email first, or contact our support team with proof of identity to manually verify your account.
