🕵️ Common fraud schemes targeting you

📧

Phishing emails / SMS

Fake messages pretending to be from banks, delivery services, or government agencies asking you to click malicious links or share passwords.

💳

Payment & Gift card scams

Scammers demand payment via gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency. Legitimate businesses never ask for these.

👤

Impersonation fraud

Fake customer support, tech support, or “family member in trouble” calls designed to steal money or data.

🖥️

Job / recruitment scams

Fake job offers asking for upfront fees, training payments, or personal bank details — a red flag.

🛡️ How to protect yourself

  • ✔️ Never share OTP, PIN, or password — even with “official” callers.
  • ✔️ Verify website URLs: look for “https://” and check for misspellings.
  • ✔️ Enable two‑factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts.
  • ✔️ Do not click on unsolicited links; visit official websites manually.
  • ✔️ Regularly monitor bank statements and credit reports.
  • ✔️ Keep devices updated and use reputable antivirus software.

⚠️ What to do if you’ve been targeted

  1. Stop all communication with the scammer immediately.
  2. Change your passwords for affected accounts (email, banking, social media).
  3. Contact your bank or credit card provider if you shared financial details.
  4. Report the fraud to local authorities and the national cybercrime helpline.
  5. Preserve evidence – screenshots, emails, call logs, transaction IDs.
