❓ Frequently asked career questions

How often should I update my resume?
Every 3–6 months, even if you're not job hunting. Add new achievements, skills, and projects while they're fresh.
Is a cover letter still necessary in 2025?
Yes, for many roles. Keep it short, tailored, and show enthusiasm. Some ATS systems still value it.
How to explain employment gaps?
Focus on what you learned during the gap (courses, freelance, volunteering). Be honest and positive.
What are the best job search platforms?
LinkedIn, Indeed, and niche boards like Wellfound (startups) or industry-specific communities.
